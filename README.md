# Room for rent near UW: 3D tour

A walkable 3D model of a private bedroom for rent and the shared spaces of a four-bedroom apartment in
Seattle's U District, near the University of Washington, built with three.js. On a computer, walk with
W/A/S/D, look around with the arrow keys or by dragging, and press E or click to open doors and
appliances; on a phone, drag to look and tap the floor to walk. This is a personal listing by a current
resident and is not affiliated with ōLiv Seattle or its management.

https://azealoo.github.io/oliv-seattle-room-tour/
